MY STORY

I am still
becoming.

This is not a biography designed to impress. It is an honest account of a life in formation — one shaped by faith, family, learning and a persistent sense that there is always more to become.

WHO I AM

More than a job title.

I am a husband, a Christian, a son, a brother, an uncle, a friend, a leader, a learner and a builder. I am also an Information Security professional and a researcher. None of these things is more important than the others — they are all part of one integrated person trying to live well and faithfully.

I grew up in Nigeria, shaped by family, faith and a culture that values education, hard work and community. Those early influences have stayed with me — in how I approach work, relationships and responsibility.

I came to faith in Christ through a personal conviction rather than cultural inheritance. That commitment is the foundation from which I try to understand everything else — including what it means to lead, serve, build and love well.

I became a husband, and that single change has been the most formative experience of my adult life. Marriage has taught me things I could not have learned any other way — about patience, service, communication and the kind of daily commitment that transforms a person gradually and without fanfare.

Professionally, I took an unusual path. I began in commercial banking and sales, moved into product management, and transitioned into information security and data protection. Each stage built something the next one needed — commercial literacy, product understanding, then governance and risk.

I am now building things beyond my formal employment — exploring ideas at the intersection of information security, governance and the Nigerian digital context. I am also a postgraduate researcher, a volunteer, and someone who believes that professional skill should be placed in service of others, not only in service of a career.

FORMATION

The things that made me.

Faith

A personal commitment to follow Christ that predates most of my professional formation. It is the lens through which I understand integrity, responsibility, service and what I owe to others.

Family

Parents who believed in education and worked hard to make it possible. Siblings who shaped my character. And a wife who has taught me more about becoming a good person than I expected anyone could.

Education

A BSc in Business Administration from the University of Ilorin. Now an MSc in Information Management at Ahmadu Bello University — chosen because learning is not something I want to stop doing.

PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

An evolution, not a sequence of unrelated jobs.

Three distinct phases that built on each other in ways I did not fully plan but can now see clearly in hindsight.

2019 – 2021

Commercial & Banking

Customer-facing roles at Wema Bank. The commercial experience — understanding how customers think, what trust means in a digital product context, how organisations build and lose credibility — is something I draw on constantly in current governance work.

2021 – 2023

Product Management

Product Manager at Heritage Bank, leading agency banking platforms. Building products requires you to sit at the intersection of business requirements, technical constraints, user needs and organisational risk simultaneously — which is exactly where governance work also lives.

2023 – PRESENT

Information Security & Data Protection

Information Security Analyst, Acting DPO, Third-Party Risk Analyst and now DPO at Digital Health Platforms. Building governance functions, compliance frameworks and assurance programmes across regulated environments.
