WHAT I'M BUILDING

CyberÀṣà

A personal venture at the intersection of information security, data protection and the Nigerian cultural and organisational context.

CyberÀṣà — from the Yoruba àṣà, meaning culture, tradition, or custom — is built on a single belief: that effective security governance must be culturally intelligent, not only technically correct. A policy that ignores how an organisation actually works will not be followed. A framework that ignores local context will not be sustained.

The vision is to develop security and data protection resources, tools and services that are genuinely useful to Nigerian and West African organisations — not transplanted from other contexts, but grown from a real understanding of the environment.

This is early-stage work. The ideas are clearer than the products. But the commitment is real and the direction is set.

GOVERNANCE

Compliance is not the same thing as security.

Passing a security assessment means an organisation met a standard at a point in time. It does not mean the organisation is resilient. The gap between the two is where most security problems live.

CULTURE

Why security governance needs organisational culture.

Controls are implemented by people. Policies are followed — or not — by people. Culture determines how consistently security practice survives the pressure of real-world business operations.

DIGITAL HEALTH

What digital trust means in health technology.

When systems process health information, trust is not optional. It is the condition under which patients agree to be known by a digital system — and that agreement can be lost faster than it is built.
