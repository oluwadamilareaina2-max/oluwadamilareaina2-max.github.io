FAITH

Following Christ.
Imperfectly. Persistently.

Faith is not a compartment of my life. It is the foundation from which everything else flows — my understanding of responsibility, leadership, service and what it means to be human.

"Before I lead well,
I must learn to follow well."
— A conviction I'm still learning

FOLLOWING CHRIST

What I believe.

I am a Christian — not because faith is culturally expected, but because I have made a personal commitment to follow Jesus Christ. That commitment shapes how I think about integrity, responsibility, relationships, leadership and what I owe to others.

Conviction, not convention

My faith is a personal choice, not an inherited background. I follow Christ because I believe it to be true — and that conviction is something I hold with humility, knowing I am still learning what it means to live it faithfully.

Follower before leader

I believe that good leadership — in a family, team, church or organisation — begins with learning to follow well. The discipline of following Christ has taught me more about leadership than any management book I have read.

Faith and responsibility

My professional interest in governance, stewardship and accountability is not separate from my faith. Scripture consistently calls us to faithfulness with what has been entrusted to us — and I take that seriously in my work.

WHAT I'M LEARNING

Still in school.

I do not present myself as someone who has arrived spiritually. These are honest lessons I am working through.

I am learning that consistency matters more than intensity. Spiritual growth is less about dramatic moments and more about faithful daily choices — to read, to pray, to serve, to show up.

I am learning that humility is not weakness. In professional environments that reward confidence and certainty, faith consistently calls me back to the discipline of admitting what I do not know and the courage to ask for help.

I am learning that service is not an add-on to a good life. It is the shape of a good life. The question is not whether I have time to serve, but whether I am willing to arrange my priorities around what matters most.

I am learning to hold professional ambition loosely. I want to build things, grow professionally and develop in leadership. I am also learning that these desires must be submitted to a larger purpose — one that is not only about what I build for myself.

I am learning that marriage is one of God's most effective classrooms. Being a husband has taught me more about patience, service, communication and selflessness than almost anything else. I have a long way still to go.

I am learning that it is good to not be finished. The Christian life is one of continual formation — and the willingness to remain teachable, changeable and growing is itself a spiritual posture worth cultivating.

SERVICE

Giving what I have.

I believe in connecting professional skills to service — using what I know to help organisations and individuals who need it.

Church & Community

I am actively involved in my church community — in ways that are currently not for public sharing. I believe the church is one of the most important institutions on earth, and participation in it is a privilege, not an obligation.

Professional Volunteering

I have served as a volunteer Data Protection Officer for AIDA (AI in Design) — providing independent privacy advice, drafting a data privacy policy, and supporting their NYSC camp engagement from a privacy perspective. Applying professional skills in service is something I want to do more of.
