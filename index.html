06 / INSIGHTS

Ideas I'm thinking through.

Notes and reflections on information security, privacy and governance.

NOTE 01

Compliance is not the same thing as security.

Passing a security assessment means an organisation met a standard at a point in time. It does not mean the organisation is resilient. The gap between the two is where most security problems live.

NOTE 02

Why security governance needs organisational culture.

Controls are implemented by people. Policies are followed — or not — by people. Culture determines how consistently security practice survives real-world operational pressure.

NOTE 03

What digital trust means in health technology.

When digital systems process health information, trust is not a nice-to-have. It is a foundational requirement — for adoption, for accuracy and for the safety of patients whose information the system holds.
